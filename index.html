ZFC Energy Group
Independent Power Producer

ZFC Energy Group is an independent power producer with a 870+ MW development pipeline spanning wind, solar PV, and battery energy storage systems. We manage the entire development lifecycle from site origination through Ready-to-Build, securing permits, land rights, grid connections, and financing structures.

Our projects are concentrated in Peru today, with a clear expansion path into broader markets across the Americas and Europe. We are building toward 2 GW+ of installed capacity by 2030, positioning ZFC Energy as a leading independent platform in the region. Our pipeline includes BESS integration and data center infrastructure opportunities.

Fitcon Energy
Industrial Services & Infrastructure

With over 8 years serving critical infrastructure operators in Peru, Fitcon Energy delivers industrial services and equipment supply across power generation, maritime, and energy infrastructure. The company combines technical depth with operational reliability to support complex asset environments.

As the services and infrastructure backbone of ZFC Partners, Fitcon is scaling into EPC, O&M, asset management, RECs trading, grid connection services, and data center infrastructure, building a fully vertically integrated platform that supports the group's long-term asset ownership model.

About
An Independent Platform for Real Asset Investment

ZFC Partners was founded on a clear thesis: Latin America's real asset markets offer asymmetric opportunity for operators willing to commit capital, build locally, and hold for the long term.

We operate as a multi-vertical investment platform, combining long-term aligned capital with institutional rigor. Our current focus is energy infrastructure and industrial services, with vertically integrated capabilities from development through asset management.

Permanent capital. No exit horizon. We are operators and owners who deploy capital with conviction alongside select co-investment partners.
